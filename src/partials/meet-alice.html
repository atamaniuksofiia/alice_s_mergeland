Meet Alice
Alice, the protagonist, is a curious and determined adventurer with a knack for solving mysteries. Guided by her wit and courage, she explores the vast world of Mergeland to unlock its secrets and restore its beauty. Whether it’s merging magical artifacts or unraveling ancient riddles, Alice’s journey is a testament to resilience and imagination.

Character Traits

Brave, creative, and resourceful

Special Ability

Alice can summon a "Merge Boost" to help players clear challenging puzzles and uncover hidden treasures

Fun Fact

Alice keeps a magical diary where all the merging wonders and secrets are recorded for players to revisit
